Allocation assumptions & service audit

Vehicle ownership: 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 vehicles at 8.2 / 40.1 / 34.2 / 12 / 4 / 1.5%. Integer household counts are apportioned, then randomly assigned.

Battery shares and charger assignments are provisional scenario assumptions. Home Level 1 is 1.44 kW; Level 2 by battery category is 3.84 / 7.68 / 9.60 / 11.52 kW, capped by the assumed onboard AC rating. Realized battery and charging shares can vary in a small fleet.

Battery totals are nominal capacity. Plugged-in availability, SOC, travel reserves, V2G hardware and grid hosting capacity are not established here. This inventory does not add charging demand to the electrical runs.
