November 21–25, 2018 · 480 fifteen-minute intervals

Greensboro EV trading results

With 376 EVs and dynamic pricing, full trading reduced coal and gas generation by 4.40 MWh compared with charging only. The fleet ended with 3.03 MWh less energy in its batteries, and more intervals exceeded a line or transformer rating.

Prices, trades and bills

Every 15 minutes, eligible EVs enter a fresh seeded shuffle. The first quote uses current base demand, passive charging, and the previous interval’s net EV trade. Each commitment updates the quote for the next EV. Settled trades use the quote accepted by each EV. Realized generation price uses the generation mix after delivery.
